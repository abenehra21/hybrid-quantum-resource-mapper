# Phase 2 results: resource prediction

Generated by `python -m hybrid_mapper report` from `data/resources.csv`
(608 rows: 3 workload families x 2 backends,
6–120 qubits). Labels are exact transpiler outputs (optimization_level=3).
MAPE = mean |predicted − exact| / exact. R² on raw counts.

**Exact transpilation is not always cheap:** median 0.68 s, but 20 transpiles took
over 10 s (max 30 s): random/none n=26 on nighthawk_r2, random/none n=30 on nighthawk_r2, random/none n=50 on nighthawk_r2, random/none n=70 on nighthawk_r2, random/none n=100 on nighthawk_r2, random/none n=110 on nighthawk_r2, vqe/circular n=50 on heron_r3, vqe/circular n=70 on heron_r3, vqe/circular n=90 on heron_r3, vqe/circular n=110 on heron_r3, vqe/circular n=120 on nighthawk_r2, vqe/reverse_linear n=120 on nighthawk_r2. Most are circuits whose interaction graph
has no exact embedding in the coupling map (e.g. 50/70/90/110-qubit rings on heavy-hex, ~28 s vs ~1 s for rings
that embed) or dense random circuits, where Qiskit's VF2 layout search runs to its limit before routing.
0 transpiles hit the 60 s hard timeout. The predictors answer in milliseconds regardless.
